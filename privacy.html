Gradis privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Without an account

Gradis works without an account. Your quiz results and progress are saved only on your own device. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

If you create an account, we store: your username (and your Google email address if you choose to link Google), a scrambled form of your password (we never store the password itself), and your quiz results, so they can sync between your devices. We use this data only to run Gradis for you. We do not sell it and we do not share it with anyone.

Google sign-in

If you use "Continue with Google", Google tells us your email address and a unique account ID. We do not receive your Google password or any other Google data.

Advertising

Gradis may show ads after a quiz. Advertising partners such as Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technology to show ads. You can manage ad personalisation in your Google ad settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all synced results at any time in the app: Account, then "Delete my account and data". Results saved on your device stay on the device until you clear them in Settings.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact details on the Gradis website.
